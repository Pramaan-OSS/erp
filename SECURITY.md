# Security Policy

The ERP team and community take security issues seriously. To report a security issue, please email [security@frappe.io](mailto:security@frappe.io) or go through the information mentioned [here]().

You can help us make ERP and all its users more secure by following the [Reporting guidelines]().

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings. We'll endeavor to respond quickly, and will keep you updated throughout the process.
