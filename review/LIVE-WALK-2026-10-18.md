# ERP live walk — 2026-10-18

This is the per-screen index supporting DONE.md. The initial pass recorded 404 page visits
while considering all 405 sidebar URLs; aliases redirect to shared canonical pages.
Each visit saved an interactive snapshot, a screenshot and browser error/console output in
`.scratch/live-walk/`. Repeated screen labels share screenshot filenames; the JSON visit log
retains every result. All 154 create forms were subsequently opened, their tabs visited,
and at least one editable field filled without submitting those drafts.

Opening a screen is not proof that every action or financial workflow on it completes.
The principal saved-record actions and live service results are listed separately in DONE.md.
Full consent, legal registration, real payments, bulk sending and arbitrary destructive
administration were not exercised. The literal every-button requirement remains broader
than the action coverage recorded here.

The initial pass found a shared missing-filter exception in reports and an origin error
in live updates; both were fixed. Final targeted checks showed no new page errors, and
no outside resource requests, on the local map, communication, report and payment screens.
Known external documentation links were redirected to local help. The map now uses a
bundled local background and keeps coordinate entry and local drawing.
